The standard dunning ruleset destroys ₹13,96,472 per 50,000 failures versus doing nothing at all. We can measure which side of that line a merchant is on — per merchant, against a real control group.

Leading with a number that goes down. “AI recovery agent, +₹2.95L” invites “versus what?”, and the answer is only impressive once the room knows the incumbent is underwater.

Four arms, identical worlds

Every arm sees the same 50,000 failures in the same 20 worlds, paired seed by seed. Baseline retries silently and contacts nobody. Rules is the industry-standard dunning ladder. Oracle knows every outcome in advance — it is not a competitor, it is the ceiling.

Twenty minutes

Each beat exists to retire one objection. Run them in this order — the sequence is the argument.

0:00

The incumbent is underwater

Show the four-arm table. Rules nets less than doing nothing.

Retires: “isn’t this just dunning with an LLM on top?” It reframes the room from uplift to damage control, which is the only frame in which a modest gain is impressive.
2:00

Volume is not the lever

Churn per contact: Rules ₹203, us ₹81, Oracle ₹71. The Oracle contacts more customers than we do and still pays less.

Retires: “so the product is just ‘send fewer messages’?” Targeting is the lever; volume is not.
5:00

The ceiling, published

Perfect foreknowledge beats silent retry by 3.46%. That is the entire prize for anyone, ever, in this world. Anyone selling 30% uplift is quoting gross, not incremental.

Retires: “you’ll promise the moon.” We cannot be undercut on honesty about a bound we published ourselves.
8:00

The measurement rig

Randomised at customer level, after every gate. Cluster bootstrap on customers, because amount is a per-customer constant off a heavy tail. Estimator validated against planted truth: 17/20 on rate, 19/20 on revenue, against an 18/20 floor set in advance. The rate row misses, and it is in the report untouched.

Retires: “how do I know these aren’t tuned?” For a data VP the reported miss is worth more than the twenty passes.
11:30

The live path

Fire a real test-mode webhook and close the loop on screen. The policy gate is deterministic — no model, no prompt, no credentials. A model that proposes a transfer produces a REJECT; there is a test. Ten compliance codes in the gate and refused again in the executors. The issuer-degradation detector halts the agent mid-outage.

Retires: Compliance’s veto, and “an LLM is deciding money movement.”

What Finance should book

Sign, not magnitude

+₹2,95,124 per 50,000 episodes, 95% CI [₹2,61,537, ₹3,28,712], winning all 20 worlds. That is 0.095% of baseline net — say it out loud before Finance computes it.

The commercial line

The bookable delta is against Rules, not Baseline: ₹16,91,597 per 50,000 episodes. No merchant runs silent retry, so that is what a pilot actually displaces.

The ceiling caps the pitch

3.46% is the whole prize. This is a margin business, not a growth business, and the deck should never imply otherwise.

What we extrapolate: exactly one thing — the ordering of the arms and the sign of the Rules delta, as a hypothesis to be re-measured on the merchant’s own data with the same holdout.

What we refuse to extrapolate: any rupee figure multiplied by Razorpay’s failed-payment volume; per-merchant projections before that merchant’s holdout reads out; and the holdout’s own incremental figure as a business case — it measures treated episodes only and its rate estimator missed the coverage floor. If those appear in a customer deck, that is a misuse and we will say so in writing.

What is wrong with it

Smallest scope first, so the largest one lands on a foundation of demonstrated candour.

  1. It was 0/20 this morning.Argmax over the action set was our pre-registered fix. It returned 0/20 and is published as falsified. The report explicitly refuses to credit it for the later win.
  2. Most of the win is a blunt rule.At the shipped fatigue rate the term stops discriminating and effectively forbids a second contact inside the window. Graded targeting alone only ties Baseline. We bought this with restraint, not precision.
  3. Our own yardstick was wrong, and a reviewer found it.The Oracle was scored on a different objective than it optimised, understating the ceiling 2.4×. Correcting it made our result look worse, not better.
  4. The benchmark did not pass through the compliance gates.The regulatory check needs a timestamp the eval harness does not supply, so all 50,000 episodes bypassed TRAI, DLT, DND and RBI. “Compliance is code” describes the product path, not the benchmark. Gates-on benchmark is a day-30 deliverable.
  5. It is our own simulator.The world’s fatigue mechanism and the policy term that exploits it were authored in the same tree. Held-out seeds confirm noise, not structure — twenty seeds is twenty draws from one set of assumptions. This is precisely why the ask is a pilot and not a rollout. The pilot exists to kill this objection or kill the product.

Also open, and not architectural: no mandate lifecycle model, UPI Autopay absent, a full-table scan per webhook, degradation state process-local, sweep section unpopulated.

The ask

People

Two backend engineers, one data scientist at half time for experiment design, ninety days. PM at half time.

Pilot merchant

Subscription business, ₹5–50 crore annual recurring, 40k+ failed payments per quarter, card-heavy, already running a dunning ladder we can measure against, willing to hold out 5% at customer level.

Success, pre-registered

Holdout CI on net contribution excludes zero and is positive; churn per contact below the merchant’s current ladder; zero confirmed compliance violations reaching a customer; gates-on benchmark published.

Kill criteria — we shut it down ourselves, no debate

  • At day 90, the holdout confidence interval includes zero.
  • Measured churn per contact exceeds the merchant’s incumbent ladder on any 30-day window.
  • Any single confirmed regulatory violation reaching a customer.

Written down before the pilot starts, and not renegotiated afterwards.

The questions in the room

CTOWhere can a model move money?

Nowhere. The policy gate is deterministic and holds no credentials. There is a test that a proposed transfer becomes a REJECT.

VP PaymentsWhat breaks at our volume?

Full-table scan per webhook, and degradation state that a deploy orphans. Both known, both inside the ninety days, neither architectural.

VP Data ScienceWhy isn’t this overfitting to your own simulator?

It probably is, partly. Held-out seeds test noise, not structure. That is exactly what the pilot’s real holdout is for.

ComplianceDid the benchmark run through the gates?

No. The gates are live in the product path and refused twice over, but the 50,000-episode numbers bypassed them. Gates-on benchmark is a day-30 deliverable.

FinanceWhat do I book?

Nothing yet. Book the pilot’s cost. The revenue line gets written only after that merchant’s own holdout reads out.
